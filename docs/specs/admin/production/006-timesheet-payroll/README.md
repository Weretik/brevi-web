## Decisions

- What each person may do comes from the server's `permissions` (`canSetUp`, `canPay`, `canClose`, `canReopen`) and the timesheet's `canEdit`; the page never decides it alone.
- The calculation is the server's; the page shows it and the warnings as they come. Hours do not change pay and say so.
- No «all OK» marks: no «Збережено» or «Закрито» badges; a closed month gets one note with who closed it, a past open month in the year table gets «не закрито».
- «Excel» is a CSV that Ukrainian Excel opens (BOM, «;», decimal commas), like «Склад тканин».
- Nothing moves from the old «Цех»: its export has no hours, payments or closed months.
